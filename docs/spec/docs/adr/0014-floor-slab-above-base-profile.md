# Floor slab above the pinned base profile

The interior floor is a **0.8 mm slab above the top of the Gridfinity base profile**, not the profile's own top face: the floor is at 5.55 mm and the nominal interior depth is `U × 7 − 9.55` (6U: 32.45 mm). The pinned base profile, outer radii, base/lip fit, 2.0 mm walls, dividers, and lid are unchanged.

The foot-per-cell base cannot have a continuous top face. The 0.5 mm inter-cell grooves must stay open up to the plane where a baseplate's mating ridge reaches (the profile's top), and the four 3.75 mm rounded foot corners leave open voids where four cells meet. With the interior cutter starting at the profile top ([ADR-0013](0013-solid-base.md)), those grooves and corner voids became 0.5 mm slots and millimetre-scale through-holes in the floor. The "cell webs" — 0.9 × 0.2 mm strips fused along each internal grid line — were a one-solid band-aid: they capped the straight grooves only, sat 0.2 mm proud of the foot tops, and left the corners open. The 4 × 4 × 6U test body measured 28.4 mm² of open floor area and showed a 42 mm grid of foot tops and webs instead of a floor; the 1 × 1 coupon never exposed it because a single foot has no internal boundary.

Community Gridfinity bins and the FreeCAD Workbench both put a floor above the foot profile for the same reason (0.8 mm in the community reference; 2.25 mm in the Workbench's 7 mm base). 0.8 mm is chosen here: it is the reference's bottom thickness, it closes the floor with four 0.20 mm layers, it sits on the filled feet so the load path is the solid base zone, and it keeps the deepest usable interior. The cell webs are removed: the slab spans every groove and ties the interior feet to the wall on its own.

**Consequences:** the standard's floor row and interior-depth formula change (`U × 7 − 9.55`; 3U/6U/9U/12U: 11.45/32.45/53.45/74.45 mm). Divider heights are still measured from the new floor, so `height_above_floor_mm` drops by 0.8 while the absolute tops (15.0 / 28.0 / 49.0 / 70.0 mm) are unchanged; the coupon's T-divider becomes 22.45 mm above the floor with the same 28.0 mm top. The `/api/standard` panel gains a floor-slab value, and `CELL_GROOVE`, `WEB_WIDTH`, and `make_cell_webs` leave `cad/shell.py`. The printed base zone stays solid: the slab is supported by the filled feet over the whole footprint, and the only spans are the 0.5 mm grooves and the corner voids, so there is still no bridged ceiling above the base.

**Status:** accepted while the system version stays **SYS-1.0** — the print checkpoints had exercised only the 1 × 1 coupon, so this is a pre-release architecture correction of the vendored spec, not a versioned change. It amends [ADR-0013](0013-solid-base.md).
