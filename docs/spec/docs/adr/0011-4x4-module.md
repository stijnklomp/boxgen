# The 4×4 module is the maximum footprint

The largest standard body is 4 × 4 Gridfinity units (167.5 × 167.5 mm). Any footprint from 1 × 1 to 4 × 4 is supported and tiles a 4 × 4 baseplate. Bodies with the same footprint stack; mixed footprints sit side by side on a 4 × 4 plate or shelf. 4 × 4 fits common furniture well: a Kallax cube is 336 × 336 mm, so two 2 × 4 or four 2 × 2 bodies tile it almost exactly, and a single 4 × 4 body leaves ample room. A 5 × 5 footprint was rejected because it is about 1.6× the mass and print time, is less portable, and does not fit a Kallax cube cleanly.
